# References and Provenance

Peer-reviewed literature used by Ribon is maintained in [`package/docs/references.bib`](../package/docs/references.bib), and each entry is cited at the corresponding theoretical or algorithmic claim in the [complete manual](../package/docs/documentation.pdf). The Typst source for that manual is [`package/docs/documentation.typ`](../package/docs/documentation.typ).

Web-only standards, manuals, parameter archives, and provenance pages are cited as URL-bearing footnotes in the documentation rather than represented as journal articles in the bibliography.

Exact parameter-archive hashes and redistribution provenance are recorded separately in [`package/NOTICE.md`](../package/NOTICE.md), while external validation methods and corpora are documented in [`VALIDATION.md`](VALIDATION.md).
